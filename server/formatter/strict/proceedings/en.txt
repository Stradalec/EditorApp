This block is used ONLY after common_en.txt. Source classification has already been completed. Format a single reference of the selected type; do not select a different template.

TEMPLATE FROM ORIGINAL STRICT PROMPT
Surname, I. O. Article title / Authors // Proceedings title. – [City][: Publisher], [Year]. – p. x–y. – [Identifiers].

TYPE-SPECIFIC RULES
Describe the individual contribution, not the entire proceedings volume. Keep the article title and containing publication distinct using //; preserve city, publisher, pages, volume and identifiers where present. Do not invent absent fields.

MISSING DATA
Square brackets in the template indicate optional placeholders, not literal output characters. Omit absent fields and their dangling separators. Never invent data; preserve supplied details even when the short template has no dedicated placeholder. Follow the common preservation rules for identifiers, formulas and personal names.

OUTPUT
Exactly one formatted bibliographic entry, without explanation.
