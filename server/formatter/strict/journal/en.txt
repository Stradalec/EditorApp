SPECIALIZED FORMATTER — JOURNAL ARTICLE
TYPE=journal; LANG=EN

This block is used ONLY after common_en.txt. Source classification has already been completed. Format a single reference of the selected type; do not select a different template.

TEMPLATE FROM ORIGINAL STRICT PROMPT
Surname, I. O. Article title / Authors // Journal title. – [Year]. – [Vol.], No. [Issue]. – [p./P. x–y or article number]. – [DOI and other identifiers].

TYPE-SPECIFIC RULES
If no issue is provided, do not add No. If pages are absent, do not invent them. Preserve article numbers as article numbers. Keep DOI and all other identifiers. Normalize V. [number] or Volume [number] to Vol. [number]. Use No. for explicit issue markers when required by the English template. Explicit journal name expansions only: Phys. Rev. B → Physical Review B; Mater. Sci. Eng. → Materials Science and Engineering. Otherwise retain original title. Preserve page notation in original language unless a specific template rule requires adjustment.

MISSING DATA
Square brackets in the template indicate optional placeholders, not literal output characters. Omit absent fields and their dangling separators. Never invent data; preserve supplied details even when the short template has no dedicated placeholder. Follow the common preservation rules for identifiers, formulas and personal names.

OUTPUT
Exactly one formatted bibliographic entry, without explanation.
